Run the hotel. Watch its footprint too.

GreenStay brings rooms, bookings, housekeeping and billing together with a built-in sustainability module — every towel skipped and every litre saved shows up on the same dashboard as revenue and occupancy, guests earn a printable eco-certificate, and unusual water usage triggers its own alert.
